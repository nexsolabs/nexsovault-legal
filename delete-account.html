Delete your Nexso Vault account

App: Nexso Vault · Developer: Nexso Labs

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Nexso Vault and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning, tap continue, type DELETE to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and sign-in credentials) and profile.
  • Your encrypted cloud backups (metadata and images).
  • Family groups you own (they are dissolved), your memberships, your shared documents and invite codes.
  • Your AI credit balance. Unused credits are forfeited.

What is not deleted

  • Documents stored only on your device. Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove them.
  • Purchases made through Google Play, which are managed by Google. Deleting your account does not cancel or refund them.
  • Crash reports held by Firebase for a limited period (Firebase default is 90 days), which are not linked to your account in our systems.
  • Purchase event records kept for accounting and fraud prevention, no longer linked to your user ID after deletion, and kept only as long as required by law (tax and accounting).

Timeline

Account data is deleted on request without undue delay. In-app deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Residual copies in provider backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Uninstalled the app or cannot sign in?

Email support@nexsolabs.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Nexso Vault account". We will verify ownership and delete your account and data, and confirm by email without undue delay and within 30 days.
